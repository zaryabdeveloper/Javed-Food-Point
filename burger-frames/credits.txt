PIXLOOM — CREDITS

Project:
PixLoom — Video ⇄ Stills

Author / Developer:
Quang Tri Nguyen

Description:
PixLoom is a free, browser-based tool for extracting still frames from video and composing still images into video. Processing is performed locally in the user's browser.
